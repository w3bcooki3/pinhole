# Code of conduct

Pinhole is maintained by volunteers for anyone who runs AWS networks. Everyone taking part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented, is expected to:

- be respectful and assume good intent, including when disagreeing;
- keep criticism about the work, not the person;
- welcome people of every background and level of experience;
- respect privacy: never post someone else's account details, snapshots, IPs or personal information.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discriminatory language, sexual content, threats, and publishing others' private information.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**, and say that it is a conduct report. Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens

The maintainer reviews every report and may edit or remove content, and warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone who breaks these rules, in proportion to what happened.
